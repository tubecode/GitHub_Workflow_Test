### Introduction

`Hello.py` is a foundational demonstration script designed for **automated academic performance analytics**. Within an educational technology or academic administration context, the script automates the processing of student assessment data, eliminating manual grade calculations and delivering immediate insights into student achievement.

---

### High-Level Business Overview

The script serves as a lightweight performance evaluation engine focused on three key business capabilities:

1. **Automated Metrics Aggregation:**
   It standardizes multiple raw assessment scores per student into a consolidated average performance score, ensuring accurate and consistent grade processing.

2. **Top-Performer Benchmarking:**
   By dynamically evaluating overall averages across the dataset, the program identifies the top-performing student. This capability supports academic recognition programs, honor rolls, or target intervention analysis.

3. **Reporting & Insights:**
   The program outputs a clean, human-readable summary detailing individual averages and highlighting the top achiever, providing stakeholders (such as educators or administrators) with instant visibility into overall group performance.
